# STI Vio-Log

Web-based Student Violation, Community Service, QR Attendance, Parent/Guardian Communication, and Clearance Management System.

## Core Features

- Role-based authentication
- Student management
- Student QR codes
- Multi-department QR scanning
- Time-In / Time-Out
- Digital DTR
- Violation management
- Community service management
- Non-compliance monitoring
- Parent/guardian contact
- Notifications
- Student communication
- Good disciplinary standing
- Disciplinary clearance
- Enrollment verification
- Reports
- Audit logs

## Roles

- STUDENT
- DEPARTMENT_HEAD
- DO_ADMIN
- SYSTEM_ADMIN
