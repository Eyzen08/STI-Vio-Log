# Saved STI Tertiary Handbook

Edition: July 2026. Captured: 12 October 2026.

Source: https://one.sti.edu/STI-BCT_Handbook.html

The complete PDF contains all 51 required reading sections plus the welcome
and commitment pages, followed by STI's original unsigned printable commitment
form. Its 85 pages include searchable text, contents, 54 bookmarks, all 19
source tables, and the academic seal. All 1,126 checked source text blocks were
verified in their corresponding PDF sections. The commitment reference has no
active form fields and submits no acknowledgment.

The three discipline reference PDFs are unchanged copies supplied by the user.
`source-manifest.json` records section coverage and SHA-256 checksums for all
four PDFs. These assets are intentionally available at public URLs; the reader
page and portal shortcuts are limited to the two discipline staff roles.

Replace the saved edition manually when STI issues an update. Check the union
of the source navigation and required-reading checklist (including overview
pages), preserve the source text/tables/images, render and review every page,
then update the manifest, versioned URLs, edition, and capture date in the
reader. STI's issued edition remains authoritative.
