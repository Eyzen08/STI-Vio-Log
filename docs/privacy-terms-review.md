# Privacy Notice and Terms of Use: Institutional Review Draft

Prepared October 10, 2026. This is an internal review draft, not published policy, proof of approval, a consent form, or an effective contract. Mandatory acknowledgment remains disabled. Current public text receives factual storage and analytics corrections only.

## Decisions Required Before Publication

Authorized STI Global City administration and its Data Protection Officer must confirm and record:

- The campus personal information controller's legal name, address, representative and privacy contact. STI's institutional policy lists `dpo@sti.edu`; confirm its application and the campus referral procedure.
- The applicable lawful ground for each purpose, including sensitive education and disciplinary information. Account acknowledgment must not substitute for a lawful processing ground or any separately required consent.
- Retention periods or documented criteria for discipline, service, attendance, messages, guardian contacts, certificates, security logs and acknowledgments; disposal, backups, legal holds and account closure procedures.
- Whether acknowledgment must be mandatory, the approved acknowledgment statement, effective dates, and which material Terms changes require renewed acknowledgment.
- Age-appropriate notice, guardian participation and any separate consent requirements for minors. Do not infer age from College/SHS membership or treat a student's click as guardian consent.
- Provider arrangements and overseas processing, certificate-verification disclosures, automated rules, escalation review, complaint handling and applicable institutional policies.

References: [STI Data Privacy Policy](https://www.sti.edu/sti_dataprivacy.asp), [NPC right to be informed](https://privacy.gov.ph/the-right-to-be-informed/), and [Data Privacy Act implementing rules](https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules%20regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/).

## Proposed Privacy Notice

### Who Is Responsible

STI Vio-Log supports STI Global City's discipline, community service and clearance processes. The campus controller identity and authorized contact details must appear here after confirmation. This notice explains portal-specific processing and should be read with STI's institutional Data Privacy Policy.

### Information and Sources

The portal handles school-assigned account identifiers; student numbers and names; academic level, program or strand, year or grade and section; email and other contact information; guardian contact information where maintained; account avatars; disciplinary incidents and actions; community service assignments, time-in/time-out records and credited hours; clearance and certificate records; official messages and notifications; authorized officer signatures; and authentication, security, audit and policy-acknowledgment records.

Information comes from authorized school records and personnel, the account holder, school workflows, security events and, where linked, Google identity verification. Google verification supports account identity and linking; it does not authorize access to unrelated Google data. Do not submit unrelated sensitive information or other people's details without appropriate authority.

### Purposes and Processing Grounds

Information is used to authenticate approved users, secure accounts, maintain discipline records, coordinate community service, document attendance and approved credit, assess clearance, issue and verify certificates, communicate about official matters, investigate misuse and maintain accountability. The published version must state the DPO-approved ground for each processing purpose, with separate treatment of sensitive information. Optional consent-based processing requires its own specific, informed and freely given choice where applicable.

### Access, Recipients and Providers

Access follows the user's assigned school role and operational scope. Students see information made available for their own account. Authorized Discipline Office personnel, department personnel and administrators receive access needed for their official responsibilities. Guardian communications and other disclosures must follow approved school procedures and applicable requirements.

The deployed service uses Vercel for frontend hosting, Render for the backend, Supabase as PostgreSQL storage, Google for linked identity checks, and the configured email provider for authentication and official delivery. The school must confirm service agreements, safeguards, relevant provider processing and transfers outside the Philippines before publishing final provider language. Disclosure to other parties must have an appropriate basis and be limited to the necessary information.

### Certificate Verification

A person with a valid certificate-verification link can view the verification information exposed by that link, including the student's full name, masked student number, certificate number, issue date and recorded issued/revoked status. Program and completed service hours are not returned by the current public verification endpoint. Verification does not recompute current clearance eligibility. Treat the link as information that can be shared beyond the portal. The DPO must approve the disclosure and whether the verification fields need further reduction.

### Automated Rules and Human Review

Portal rules calculate attendance duration and credited service, apply service limits, summarize offense progression and identify clearance eligibility from stored records. These calculations support official workflows. Disputed records and outcomes should be reviewed by authorized personnel through the school's established process; the portal must not be described as independently making every disciplinary decision.

### Retention and Protection

The final notice must state approved retention periods or clear retention criteria for the categories above, including acknowledgments and backup copies. Some short-lived authentication data is cleaned up separately from official records. Deactivation does not necessarily remove records subject to an approved retention requirement or legal hold.

Safeguards include production encrypted connections, cookie-based authentication, request protection, role and department restrictions, protected database access, audit records and MFA for applicable accounts. No system is risk-free; promptly report suspected unauthorized access or disclosure using official channels, without including passwords or authentication codes.

### Cookies, Browser Storage and Measurement

Authentication uses a secure HTTP-only session cookie in production. Essential cookies also support CSRF protection and MFA. Local storage holds limited account display information and preferences; session storage holds request-protection and temporary workflow state. Authentication credentials are not stored in local storage. Vercel Analytics and advertising or third-party behavioral trackers are not loaded. Operational health, error, latency and security signals remain part of running the service. Any future optional tracking requires prior institutional review, an updated notice and appropriate controls.

### Your Rights and Requests

You may contact the authorized campus office or DPO to exercise applicable rights, including information, access, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, and other rights provided by Philippine law. Requests require proportionate identity and authority verification. Rights may be subject to applicable conditions, retention obligations and lawful exceptions; the notice must not promise automatic deletion of every official record. Where processing relies on consent, explain how to withdraw it and its consequences. Privacy concerns may also be raised with the National Privacy Commission through its official complaint channels.

### Students Who Are Minors

Use clear, age-appropriate explanations and allow students to seek assistance from a parent or legal guardian. The school must determine when guardian involvement or consent is required and use a separate approved process when necessary. A student acknowledgment does not establish guardian consent, waive privacy rights or provide blanket permission to process information.

### Contact and Changes

Direct privacy requests to the confirmed campus contact and STI DPO. STI's published institutional contact is `dpo@sti.edu`. Include current version, effective date and revision date. Explain significant changes through appropriate school channels. Offering this notice or recording its version does not establish consent or prove it was read.

## Proposed Terms of Use

### Authorized Users and Purpose

Use STI Vio-Log only for authorized school discipline, community service, attendance, clearance, communication and administration. Use the account assigned to you and act within its permissions. These portal terms support applicable school policies and do not replace the institution's disciplinary or appeal procedures.

### Accurate Information and Records

Provide accurate information within your authority. Authorized personnel must enter, review and correct records responsibly, use the required reasons for changes, and preserve audit history. Students may raise disputed or inaccurate records through the Discipline Office and the school's applicable review procedure. Do not falsify incidents, attendance, service credit, signatures, messages or certificates.

### Account Security

Protect your password and MFA credentials, keep recovery details accurate, sign out on shared devices and report suspected misuse promptly. Do not share accounts, impersonate others, use another person's QR or credentials, or allow another person to perform attendance actions on your behalf.

### Confidentiality, Uploads and Communication

Use student records only for authorized official purposes. Do not disclose, photograph, copy, export or redistribute confidential portal information without appropriate authority. Upload only relevant material you are authorized to provide. Messages must remain appropriate for official school work. Records, uploads and messages may be retained according to the approved retention rules described in the Privacy Notice.

### Prohibited Activity

Do not attempt unauthorized access, bypass permissions or security checks, tamper with records or verification links, upload malicious material, exploit vulnerabilities, harass others, or disrupt the portal. Report security concerns through the institution's approved channel without accessing or disclosing unrelated records.

### Attendance, Service and Clearance

Follow the assigned service and attendance procedures. Portal time calculations, approved credit and eligibility indicators depend on the recorded information and applicable school rules. Clearance approval and certificate issuance remain subject to the authorized school workflow. Raise timing errors or disputed credit promptly for audited correction rather than altering evidence.

### Availability and Access Restrictions

Maintenance, outages or security incidents may interrupt access. The school may restrict an account to address misuse, compromised credentials or other authorized administrative reasons, following applicable institutional procedures. Contact the Discipline Office for assistance with access or time-sensitive obligations.

### Privacy, Minors and Acknowledgment

The Privacy Notice explains how information is handled. Terms acknowledgment records that the authenticated user acknowledged the displayed Terms; it is not blanket consent, a waiver of statutory rights, proof that every section was read, or guardian consent. Minors may seek assistance, and any legally or institutionally required guardian involvement must use the approved separate process. Administration and DPO must approve whether acknowledgment is mandatory and the final wording.

### Changes and Review

Each released document has a version and revision date. Require renewed acknowledgment only for approved material Terms changes, without repeating it on normal logins. Questions or disputes should use the official school contact and review procedures. Do not add liability waivers, penalties, governing-law commitments or guardian attestation without institutional approval.

## Technical Release and Activation Record

The technical release publishes factual corrections and improved links, removes analytics loading and prepares disabled acknowledgment enforcement. No institutional approval is asserted. Implementation preserves versioned snapshots, derives acknowledgment identity and time on the server, stores hashes of the documents offered, prevents normal runtime edits/deletion, and denies direct public database access.

For activation, retain written approval with the exact content versions, dates, acknowledgment statement, retention decision, minors procedure and mandatory-access decision. Add approved immutable snapshots and update the shared manifest in a separately tested release. Never rewrite an already released snapshot. Editorial edits receive a new content version but retain the acknowledgment version; approved material Terms changes receive a new acknowledgment version. Privacy Notice updates alone do not require renewed Terms acknowledgment.

For rollback, disable enforcement and redeploy while preserving records. Owner-controlled erasure or retention changes need the approved institutional procedure; normal runtime access cannot delete acknowledgment evidence.
